# DeFi capability operations and limitations

The assembled catalog contains 108 active, source-attributed function capabilities across seven mainnets: 88 actions and 20 independently grantable canonical `approve(address,uint256)` functions. It preserves the original 70 definitions and adds 38 (36 actions, 2 approvals): 14 Aerodrome/Velodrome classic-router calls, 10 PancakeSwap V2 calls, 6 Morpho Blue calls, and 8 Lido calls (6 actions, 2 approvals). Admission is exact to chain, contract address, selector, and fixed ABI. Provenance records official address/interface sources and dates; it is not a protocol safety, liquidity, runtime-identity, or transaction-success guarantee. Coverage is limited to these named protocol/version entries, not “most DeFi.” See [`docs/defi-capability-matrix.md`](../../../docs/defi-capability-matrix.md), the [baseline provenance inventory](../../../docs/defi-research/simple-catalog-provenance.md), [DEX expansion evidence](../../../docs/defi-research/expansion-dex-provenance.md), and [lending/staking expansion evidence](../../../docs/defi-research/expansion-lending-staking-provenance.md).

Each API key must receive explicit capability-ID grants; default grants remain empty. A grant authorizes the cataloged function, while the caller controls its ABI arguments—including amounts, assets, paths, recipients, borrow parameters, minimum output, deadlines, and any native value accepted by payable functions. No protocol-specific financial, slippage, recipient, price, health, or amount cap is added. Protocol-native revert semantics remain unchanged. `approve(address,uint256)` is an independent grant: any spender and any `uint256` amount, including the maximum, are permitted. It is never auto-granted with an action and has no matching-action, cleanup, or reset requirement. Catalog admission does not grant access. Existing capability IDs are preserved; owners should review prior grants and revoke/re-grant if they do not intend the broader scope.

DeFi signing remains disabled; Openfort TEE remains the signing custody boundary. Unknown functions, ungranted capabilities, paused capabilities, and unsupported broad execution surfaces remain denied. IAM-only catalog/grant management, step-up for grant edits, API-key permissions, freezes, live pause and grant rechecks, session-key readiness, EOA isolation, and destination/billing gates remain independent controls. Generic transaction constraints and policies also remain: configured API-key spend budgets with UTC/native-wei accounting, structural limits of 10 interactions and 100 grants, Permit/Permit2 and NFT operator restrictions, destination protection, billing-debt policy, idempotency, and simulation. These separate controls may reject an otherwise granted function; they do not impose protocol-specific financial caps. No grants are automatically issued or backfilled, and catalog growth does not change the 100-grant limit.

Pause state is persisted in the singleton `defi_policy_state` row keyed by `global`. `DefiPauseService.setPaused(scope, paused, operator)` accepts structured `global`, chain, contract, or capability scopes and records actual changes transactionally. Pause remains an overlay on explicit grants. The database recheck cannot revoke an already signed or submitted operation.

Catalog construction validates fixed ABI identity and source/date provenance, merges family fragments, and deep-freezes the result. DeFi authorization checks exact catalog function/grant/pause state and canonical ABI decoding; it does not run separate financial templates or execution-evidence policy. Allowed events are exported after commit; denial audit follows rollback and must not mask the denial.

**Validation status:** Oracle Gate 1 initial attempt 1/3 passed for scoped catalog admission and local authorization, with no material finding or re-review required. This is not a protocol-safety, runtime/code-identity, liquidity, deployment, or execution-success proof. No funded live UserOperation result is claimed, and authenticated browser verification is unverified. Lido withdrawal requests are asynchronous and require protocol finalization before claims. Morpho Blue entries cover selected borrow/withdraw calls but omit supply, supplyCollateral, and repay callbacks, so they do not provide a complete position-creation workflow.
