# QuickSwap V2 registry fixture

Builds a standalone, source-qualified Polygon PoS V2 router fragment for offline manifest and policy tests. This source candidate has not been admitted or assembled into production. It authorizes only ten ordinary swap/liquidity selectors at the exact documented router, with explicit API-key capability grants; caller-selected arguments remain uncapped. No pool inventory, execution, deployment-bytecode or liquidity claim is made.
