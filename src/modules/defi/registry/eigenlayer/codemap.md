# EigenLayer StrategyManager and DelegationManager fixture

An explicit test-only fragment for three nonpayable calls on the pinned Ethereum StrategyManager and DelegationManager proxy roles: strategy deposit, queue withdrawal, and complete withdrawal. The raw v6 source remains inactive; `buildEigenLayerRegistry()` is active only in isolated Catalog/Policy tests and is not production-wired or granted. Strategy address/eligibility and token choice are caller arguments, not a platform allowlist. Withdrawal queues remain subject to protocol delay/slashing and the protocol's own staker/withdrawer completion semantics. The separately listed stETH strategy is deployment metadata, not a separate capability. This fixture does not certify a complete/funded flow, live proxy implementation, liquidity, or eligible strategy population. See [source review](../../../../../docs/defi-research/expansion55-eigenlayer-v6.md).
