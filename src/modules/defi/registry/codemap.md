# DeFi registry

Registry contracts are immutable fragments of nested `chain -> contract -> function` capability data. A reviewed manifest exposes `{ chains, capabilities, manifestHash }`; `buildReviewedManifest` rejects duplicate IDs/selectors and inconsistent hierarchy/ABI, validates active identity/provenance, and deep-freezes the result. Committed production baseline v4 remains 368 definitions (345 actions, 23 separately grantable approvals) and 14 scopes; the 12 literal profiles are unchanged. The explicit local FIRST20/v5 assembly staging adds 138 exact source-bound functions, no approvals, and one Ambient scope for totals 506 and 15, while preserving all old v4 definitions. This staged static fragment is pending parent validation and the FIRST20 review gate, not deployment or a grant. Source admission does not prove runtime code, financial safety, market eligibility, liquidity, or funded execution.
