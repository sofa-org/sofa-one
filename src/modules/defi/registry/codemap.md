# DeFi registry

Registry contracts are immutable fragments of nested `chain -> contract -> function` capability catalog data. A reviewed manifest exposes exactly `{ chains, capabilities, manifestHash }`; no deployment, asset, market, pool, feed, or runtime-evidence inventory is part of authorization. `buildReviewedManifest` merges family fragments, rejects duplicate IDs/selectors and inconsistent hierarchy/ABI, validates fixed ABI identity and source/date provenance, and deep-freezes a cloned result. Current production v4 has 368 definitions (345 actions, 23 separately grantable approvals) across seven aggregate chain IDs. It preserves all 349 v3 definitions and adds 19 source-qualified functions (Curve 4, PancakeSwap 9, Yearn 6). Fourteen total scopes include the unchanged 13 v3 scopes plus one finite PancakeSwap same-target multicall scope; its eight children are fixed and individually granted. Twelve version 1.0.0 profiles contain 94 unique exact IDs, including three new bounded function selections; none automatically assigns grants. Gate 4 passed finally on attempt 3/3 with R1/R2/R3 closed. The M4 partial delivery has no claimed commit hash; its independent coverage result remains `FALSE`, with no supported market denominator. Source admission is not deployment, runtime-code identity, liquidity, financial validation, or funded-execution proof.
