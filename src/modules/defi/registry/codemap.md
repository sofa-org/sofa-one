# DeFi registry

Registry contracts are immutable fragments of nested `chain -> contract -> function` capability catalog data. A reviewed manifest exposes exactly `{ chains, capabilities, manifestHash }`; no deployment, asset, market, pool, feed, or runtime-evidence inventory is part of authorization. `buildReviewedManifest` merges family fragments, rejects duplicate IDs/selectors and inconsistent hierarchy/ABI, validates fixed ABI identity and source/date provenance, and deep-freezes a cloned result. Active entries require verified source provenance; unverified entries remain inactive. ABI identity is Keccak-256 of recursively key-sorted ABI JSON (array order preserved). Catalog identity hash sorts capability IDs and binds ID/type/chain/normalized address/signature/ABI hash/status while excluding descriptions and provenance dates. Production assembly preserves the 70-capability baseline and adds 38 definitions (36 actions, 2 approvals), for 108 active functions total (88 actions, 20 separately grantable canonical approvals). Oracle Gate 1 initial attempt 1/3 passed for scoped catalog admission and local authorization, with no material finding or re-review required.
