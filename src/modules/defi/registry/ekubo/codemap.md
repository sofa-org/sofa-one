# Ekubo Ethereum Positions fixture

Isolated Catalog/Policy test fixture for six payable methods on the documented Ethereum Original v3.1.1 Positions deployment: mint-and-deposit, deposit, two withdraw overloads, and two collect-fees overloads. Overloaded capability IDs include computed selector suffixes. Raw source remains inactive and this builder is not runtime-wired or automatically granted. It is a bounded LP action subset, not swaps or a full cross-chain workflow. See [source review](../../../../../docs/defi-research/expansion55-ekubo-v6.md).
