## Inspected call behavior and limits

The inherited two-argument `mint` is `nonReentrant` and `settleInterest`. Its fixed path calls `controller.beforeMint`, obtains the exchange rate, and calls the configured underlying token's `safeTransferFrom` from `msg.sender` for `_mintAmount`. It calculates iTokens from the actual received amount, mints to caller-selected `_recipient`, checks `TOTAL_SUPPLY_THRESHOLD`, calls `controller.afterMint`, and emits `Mint`. Controller, underlying-token behavior and configuration, rate/interest model, caller balance/allowance, market totals, threshold, and other protocol conditions can affect or revert execution. A distinct ERC-20 allowance may be needed; the platform does not pair or grant an approval.

The inherited two-argument `redeem` is also `nonReentrant` and `settleInterest`. It calculates the underlying amount using the current interest-dependent exchange rate and enters `_redeemInternal`, which rejects zero iToken shares, invokes `controller.beforeRedeem`, burns `_from`'s iTokens, checks the supply threshold, calls `_doTransferOut(msg.sender, amount)`, invokes `controller.afterRedeem`, and emits `Redeem`. `_burnFrom` requires token allowance if `_from` differs from `msg.sender`; the platform neither restricts `_from` to the execution owner nor creates that allowance.

For this V2 inheritance chain, `_doTransferOut` is overridden: it calls `controller.beforeTransferUnderlying(iToken, configuredUnderlying, amount, msg.sender)`, takes the returned `_dst`, then transfers the configured underlying to `_dst`. Thus the caller is supplied to the configured controller hook, but the hook selects the actual destination. The payout may be redirected by configured controller behavior; it is not guaranteed to reach either `msg.sender` or `_from`. Controller routing, permissions, market accounting/cash, underlying token, interest/exchange rate, balance/allowance, threshold, and protocol conditions may redirect or revert. These are protocol trust and execution risks, not new platform recipient gates or execution steps.

The platform leaves `_recipient` and `_from` caller-selected (including zero or an address distinct from the execution owner) and leaves the uint256 arguments unrestricted; protocol validation may reject such calls. No amount, recipient, owner-equality, balance, oracle, or financial-safety gate is added. Only these two methods are admitted: controller market entry/admin functions, borrow/liquidation/flash-loan, `redeemUnderlying`, the three-argument refreshEligibility wrappers, permit, routers, and native-ETH helpers are excluded. No automatic grant, approval pairing, profile, or execution scope is added.
