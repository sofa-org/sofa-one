The scope accepts path 1's exact 320-byte static tuple `(address,address,uint256,bool,bool,uint128,uint16,uint128,uint128,uint8)` and path 2's exact 352-byte tuple `(uint8,address,address,uint256,int24,int24,uint128,uint128,uint128,uint8,address)`. Path 2 accepts only LP commands `1,2,3,4,11,12,21,22,31,32,41,42`. Other paths, commands, malformed encodings, and nested wallet execution are excluded. The 12 LP codes are a bounded reviewed fixture grammar, not a claim that the cited swap page documents each LP code, all Ambient support, or pool coverage.

All ABI-valid financial fields—including token addresses, pool identity, quantities, ticks, flags, tips, limits and conduit—remain caller choices, with no financial caps or allowlists. LP conduit callbacks are the protocol's fixed `depositCrocLiq` / `withdrawCrocLiq` operations delegated by the DEX sidecar; they are not arbitrary wallet calldata or child interactions. External LP conduits create counterparty/asset-loss risk; sidecars may be governably upgraded. Pool eligibility, liquidity and funded execution are not assured. The root's native value is counted once under existing policy budgets. Approval or user-grant assignment is not implied.
