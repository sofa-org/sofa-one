## Ordinary call behavior and limits

The inspected implementation converts deposit/mint amounts through its exchange-rate path, then validates the receiver, taker roles and deposit cap before pulling the configured asset from `msg.sender` and minting shares. Withdraw/redeem convert caller-selected shares/assets, check and burn the selected owner's shares, spend existing LP-share allowance when owner differs from caller, and push the configured asset to the selected receiver subject to vault liquidity. Protocol cap, roles, share balance/allowance, zero-address checks, and available liquidity can reject calls. Underlying-asset approval and third-party share allowance remain independent protocol prerequisites; this update creates no approval pairing or grants.

Amounts and receiver/owner values remain caller-selected, including ABI-valid zero or maximum uint256 values; this platform adds no financial cap, recipient/owner equality, balance, or price-feed gate. Product documentation describing USDS backing does not establish initialized asset storage for this target. Registry/source evidence does not establish current proxy implementation/runtime, initialized asset value, live funding/liquidity, successful execution, or financial safety. No method admission is an automatic grant or complete-workflow guarantee.
