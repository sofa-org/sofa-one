## Ordinary call behavior and limits

The inherited Solmate ERC4626 deposit/mint paths use the vault's fixed immutable ERC-20 `asset`, transfer it from `msg.sender`, mint shares to the caller-selected receiver, then update AutoPxEth staking accounting. Deposit rejects a zero-share rounding result. Withdraw/redeem use selected owner shares and caller-selected receiver; if caller and owner differ, existing share allowance is checked/spent. They invoke AutoPxEth's withdrawal hook, burn shares, and transfer the configured ERC-20 asset. The hook may harvest when the requested assets exceed `totalStaked`; harvest sends the configured platform fee to the platform address from the fixed asset, then stakes the remainder. Withdrawal penalty and reward/accounting calculations affect previews and returned amounts.

These methods are nonpayable. The `pxETH` product label does not establish the constructor's configured asset value or imply native-ETH wrapping. Token balance/allowance, zero-share/zero-asset rounding, withdrawal penalty, rewards/accounting, and available liquidity can affect or revert execution. The platform adds no amount caps, owner/receiver equality, balance/feed gates, automatic grants, or approval pairing. ERC20 transfer/transferFrom Pirex redemption hooks are outside this four-function admission. Dated product/source records do not establish current runtime or constructor asset configuration, funding, liquidity, successful execution, or financial safety.
