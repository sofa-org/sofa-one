## Inspected deposit behavior and limitations

The address-bound `depositETH(address receiver)` body checks `depositsPaused`, rejects `msg.value == 0`, calls `previewDeposit(msg.value)`, rejects zero computed shares, mints the shares to the caller-selected receiver, increments `totalDepositedInPool`, and emits `Deposit`. `previewDeposit` calls `_convertToShares`: when total supply is zero the conversion is 1:1; otherwise it uses `Math.mulDiv` with total supply and `totalAssets()`. `totalAssets()` adds the pool's `totalDepositedInPool` to `totalDeposited()`, which calls the configured `StakingNodesManager`. Share conversion therefore depends on the protocol's accounting and configured manager; pause, zero-value, zero-share, receiver/mint, conversion, manager, arithmetic, or other protocol conditions can revert.

Platform authorization leaves payable native value within its full uint256 range and leaves the ABI receiver caller-selected, including zero or an address different from the execution owner. It adds no amount cap, receiver/owner equality, balance, manager, oracle, recipient, or financial gate. Consequently policy acceptance of zero value or a particular receiver is not a claim that protocol execution succeeds. The source's direct `receive()` reverts; `withdrawETH(uint256)` is restricted to the configured StakingNodesManager. No withdrawal, receive/fallback, administrator/manager, ynETHx, zap, permit, router, batch, or restaking-management function is admitted. This deposit capability alone is not a complete exit workflow.
